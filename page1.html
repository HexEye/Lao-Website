About Lao Language

Lao is a human-friendly, simple, and powerful scripting language built on top of Lua. It was designed to make programming feel as natural as human thinking.
Lightweight & Fast

Built on top of the robust Lua engine, Lao inherits lightning-fast execution speed and extreme portability across multiple platforms. It maintains a minimal resource footprint, making it ideal for everything from low-end embedded systems to high-performance game development. By optimizing internal bytecode execution, Lao ensures that your applications run smoothly without draining system resources or adding unnecessary overhead to your codebase.

Human-Friendly

Say goodbye to rigid, cryptic, and overly complex syntax that slows down your workflow. Lao introduces an incredibly readable and elegant syntax designed to let you focus entirely on your logic and creativity. By mimicking natural language structures, it reduces the steep learning curve for beginners while providing seasoned developers with a clean, highly expressive toolset to turn ideas into functional code effortlessly.

Open Source & MIT

Lao is completely free, community-driven, and proudly published under the permissive MIT License. This means you have total freedom to use, modify, distribute, and even sell your projects built with Lao without any restrictions or hidden fees. Our goal is to empower developers, students, and hobbyists worldwide with an accessible, completely transparent ecosystem where anyone can contribute to the future of the language.
