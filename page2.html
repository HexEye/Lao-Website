Meet the Developer

Hi! I am the creator and independent developer behind the Lao Language project. Driven by a deep passion for software modification, custom scripting engine design, and reverse engineering, I built Lao to make coding more intuitive. My goal is to combine the lightweight efficiency of Lua with a deeply readable, human-friendly syntax. When I'm not crafting language tools, I focus on analyzing systems and exploring visual software structures.
